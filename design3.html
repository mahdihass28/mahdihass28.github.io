Design 3: E-commerce Site

An online store with product listings and a shopping cart

Project Overview

This design will be an e-commerce website where users can browse products, add items to a cart, and proceed to checkout. The focus is on a clean shopping experience with intuitive navigation.

Planned Features

  • Product catalogue with search and filter
  • Individual product detail pages
  • Shopping cart with quantity controls
  • Checkout form (no real payment processing)
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop

Design Goals

Visual hierarchy will direct attention to products and calls-to-action. A consistent colour palette and whitespace will keep the interface clean and trustworthy.
