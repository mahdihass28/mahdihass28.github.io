Canadian Food Price Dashboard

A bilingual interactive dashboard visualizing Canadian retail food prices

Open the dashboard

Project Overview

This bilingual interactive dashboard visualizes how Canadian retail food prices evolved between 2021 and 2025. It is a high-fidelity prototype developed in React with Recharts for Devoir 5 of SEG3525, applying the Module 8 concepts of visualization (the 3Cs) and internationalization. The dataset is synthetic, generated with AI and modeled on Statistics Canada food-price trends, and the dashboard states this clearly.

Two chart types, fully interactive

Line chart — price evolution: monthly price of a chosen product, with an optional second product to compare, and a year-range filter (2021–2025). A crosshair tooltip reveals exact values for any month.
Bar chart — product comparison: all eight products ranked for any chosen month and year, with horizontal bars so long French labels stay readable.
Global controls: a CAD/USD currency toggle re-prices every chart and KPI, and three KPI tiles summarize the basket cost, its 12-month change, and the fastest-rising product.

Internationalization

  • Full English/French language switcher, designed as labeled buttons (no flags)
  • Every string localized: titles, menus, axis labels, legends, tooltips
  • Locale-aware number, currency and date formats via Intl (e.g. $5.61 vs 5,61 $)
  • The document language and page title update with the selected language

Design choices

  • Light neutral theme so the data carries the visual weight (clutter-free)
  • Colorblind-safe two-series palette, validated for CVD separation and contrast
  • One accent hue per series; muted grid and axes keep contrast on the data
  • Responsive layout: side-by-side chart cards that stack on narrow screens
