Project Overview

Prestige Cuts is a barbershop services website built for a local Ottawa business. The site helps clients discover services, meet the team, and book an appointment online.

Features

  • Hero section with direct booking call-to-action
  • Services grid: Classic Cut, Skin Fade, Beard Trim, Combo, Kids Cut with pricing
  • 4-step interactive booking form (service, date/time, contact info, confirmation)
  • Team section with 3 barber profiles
  • Contact section with address, hours, and phone

Design Decisions

Dark background (#111) with gold accents (#c8a45a) establishes a premium feel. Oswald headings pair with Inter body text for contrast between display and reading sizes. The booking flow breaks a multi-field form into 4 labeled steps to reduce cognitive load.

Status: Complete. Built with React + Vite.
